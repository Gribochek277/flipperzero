marauder_sniff: expose the firmware's -d (aggressive deauth) — gated hard
A forced handshake capture needs the device to transmit deauths so the AP's
clients reconnect; that is the firmware's `sniffpmkid -d`. The tool did not
expose it, so `mode=deauth` (which only LISTENS for deauth frames) was the
closest thing — and it forces nothing.

- extension/marauder-tools.ts: `aggressive` (boolean) on marauder_sniff.
  Gates: pmkid only, an explicit `channel` is mandatory, and it gets its own
  attack-class dialog that names the channel, states that the Marauder WILL
  transmit deauth frames and that those clients get disconnected. Wire:
  `sniffpmkid -c <ch> -d`. The report header says AGGRESSIVE (deauth TX) and
  echoes the firmware's own `DEAUTH TX:` line.
- dev/fake_marauder.py: parses `-d`, emits `DEAUTH TX: TRUE/FALSE`.
- dev/test-marauder-ops.ts: +11 checks — `-d` on the wire, dialog is
  attack-class and names the deauth TX, non-pmkid rejected, missing channel
  rejected, declined -> NOTHING sent (no deauths). 52/52.
- README/docs/protocol.md: aggressive documented (receive-only everywhere
  else in this tool).
